About PetroChem Match

Our Mission

To revolutionize B2B networking in the oil & gas industry by connecting professionals, operators, engineers, suppliers, and consultants on a global platform. We believe that great innovations happen when the right people meet.

What We Offer

  • Smart Matching: AI-powered compatibility scoring to find your ideal collaborators
  • Professional Directory: Search and filter by role, expertise, location, and experience
  • RFQ Hub: Post and respond to requests for quotes seamlessly
  • Engineering Tools: ESG calculator, chemical lookup, and unit converter
  • Secure Platform: Enterprise-grade encryption with Supabase

Built For Industry Professionals

Whether you're an operator managing offshore assets, a chemical engineer optimizing processes, a specialty supplier showcasing products, or a consultant providing expertise—PetroChem Match connects you with the right opportunities.

Our Technology

Built with Flask, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Tailwind CSS, our platform is designed for reliability, speed, and user experience. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
